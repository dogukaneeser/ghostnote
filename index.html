GhostNote · Support

Handwrite over videos & websites

GhostNote is a note-taking app for iPad and Apple Pencil that lets you write right on top of lecture videos, tutorials, articles and research pages while you watch and read. The video keeps playing under a transparent ink layer and your handwriting lands straight in your notebook, so you never pause to switch apps. There is no account: your notes stay on your iPad.

  • iPad · iPadOS 18 or later
  • No account
  • No ads, no tracking
  • Free with 3 notebooks

What you can do

  • Write over videos and websites

    Open a lecture, tutorial, article or paper in the built-in browser and write on it with Apple Pencil. The video keeps playing under your ink, and your finger still scrolls and taps the page.

  • Snips that keep their source

    Capture part of a page or video into your notebook. Each snip keeps its link and video time, so you can jump back to that exact moment.

  • Real notebooks

    Continuous scrolling with blank, lined, dotted, grid, isometric, music and Cornell paper. Five pens, lasso, shape snapping, stickers, images, text boxes and sticky notes.

  • PDFs and PDF Studio

    Write on PDFs, sign, fill in forms and scan documents. PDF Studio merges, splits, compresses, crops, redacts and password-protects files (some advanced options need Pro).

  • Study tools and search

    Flashcards, focus timer, calculator and voice notes with on-device transcription. One search across handwriting, typed text, PDFs, snips and transcripts.

  • Private by design

    No account, no ads, no tracking. On iPads that support Apple Intelligence, summaries, quizzes and “Ask my notes” run on the device, and your notes never leave it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write on a video or a website?
  1. In the library, tap New and add a website when you create the notebook. You can type an address, or any other text to search for it.
  2. Write with Apple Pencil. Your finger still scrolls, taps and plays the video underneath, so you don't need to switch modes.
  3. Use the Halo, the small floating control: tap its centre button to switch between Write and Browse (handy if you write with your finger). Hold it for a quick menu with capture, focus mode and dim video, which makes your ink stand out.
  4. Hide the web at any time to see the same notes on a clean notebook page.

To keep a moment, use capture: the snip lands in your notebook with its link and video time.

Do I need an account? Where are my notes stored?

There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you create is saved only in GhostNote's private storage on this iPad. GhostNote has no server of its own and there is no iCloud sync yet, so your notes don't appear on your other devices, and we can't see or recover them for you.

Please keep your own copies of notebooks that matter:

  • Export a notebook as a PDF, PNG files or images, then choose Save to Files to keep it in a folder of your choice. In the library you can select several notebooks and export them at once (one PDF per notebook).
  • If your iPad is backed up to iCloud Backup or to a computer, iPadOS may include GhostNote's data in that backup, depending on your device settings.

Deleting GhostNote from your iPad removes all of its notes from the device, so export first. Notebooks you delete inside the app go to Recently Deleted for 30 days before they are removed for good.

What's free, and what does GhostNote Pro add?

Free

  • 3 notebooks with unlimited pages
  • Every pen and tool, writing over videos and websites
  • Handwriting search
  • Exports with no watermark
  • 60 minutes of transcription and 5 assistant actions a month

Pro

  • Unlimited notebooks
  • Unlimited transcription and assistant actions
  • Search by meaning
  • Two notebooks side by side
  • All planners and custom templates

Pro is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. Pro works with Family Sharing: up to 5 family members can use it at no extra cost.

If Pro ends, nothing is deleted or locked: every notebook still opens and can be exported. The assistant features need an iPad that supports Apple Intelligence.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Then open the library sidebar and go to Settings › Account & Pro and tap Restore Purchases. The same button is on the Pro screen.

If a family member shared Pro with you through Family Sharing, it usually appears on its own; tap Restore Purchases if it doesn't. Have an offer or promo code? Use Redeem Code in the same place.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPad, open the Settings app and go to your name › Subscriptions › GhostNote Pro, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from GhostNote: Settings › Account & Pro › Manage subscription.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment, so there is nothing to cancel. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which iPads are supported?

GhostNote is made for iPad and needs iPadOS 18 or later. It's designed for Apple Pencil; you can also turn on finger drawing.

The on-device AI features (lecture-video summaries, summaries and quizzes from your notes, suggested flashcards and “Ask my notes”) appear only on iPads that support Apple Intelligence, with it turned on and available in your language and region. On other iPads these items are hidden and everything else works.

I deleted a notebook by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Deleted notebooks move to Recently Deleted in the library, where you can restore them. After 30 days, or if you choose Delete forever or Empty trash, they are gone for good.

Does GhostNote track me?

No. GhostNote has no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs. Websites you open in the built-in browser are loaded directly from those sites under their own privacy policies. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Report a problem or ask a question

Support questions and bug reports are handled on GitHub. Open an issue and tell us what happened, which iPad model and iPadOS version you use, and your GhostNote version (shown in Settings › About).

Issues are public and need a free GitHub account. Please don't include personal information, private notes or screenshots of anything private.
