GhostNote: Video & Web Notes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Effective date: 30 October 2026

GhostNote is a note-taking app for iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your notes stay on your iPad. GhostNote has no account, no server of its own, no analytics or tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

1.Information stored on your device

Everything you create in GhostNote is saved only in the app's private storage on your iPad:

  • notebooks, pages, handwriting and drawings (Apple PencilKit), text boxes, sticky notes and flashcards;
  • images you add, page snips (captures) of websites together with the address they came from, scanned documents and imported PDFs;
  • voice notes you record;
  • folders, favourites, covers, the in-app browsing history (used for address suggestions and the “Sources” list), study statistics such as focus minutes, and your settings;
  • files you work on in PDF Studio.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

If your iPad is backed up (to iCloud Backup or to a computer), iPadOS may include the app's data in that backup according to your device settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by GhostNote.

2.The in-app browser

GhostNote contains a web browser (Apple WebKit) so you can take notes next to a website or video.

  • Websites you open are loaded directly from those websites. They receive what any browser sends (for example your IP address and the pages you request) and may set cookies or store data, under their own privacy policies.
  • Cookies, cache and other website data are kept on your iPad by WebKit.
  • When you type text that is not a web address into the address bar or into the “website” field of a new notebook, it is sent as a search to Google (google.com), just as if you had searched there yourself.
  • Site icons. To show the real icons of your favourites and of sites in your history and tabs, GhostNote downloads each site's icon directly from that website: it reads the icon the page itself names (from a page you open, or from the site's home page for a favourite you have not opened yet) or the site's standard icon file (such as /favicon.ico). These requests go only to that website (or to the address the website gives for its icon) and carry no cookies or account information. No third-party icon service is used. The icons are stored only in the app's cache on your iPad and are refreshed about once a month. Clearing website data in Settings › Browser also removes these icons.
  • GhostNote checks whether your iPad is online only to show an “offline” message; nothing is sent for this.
  • You can clear the in-app browsing history and all website data at any time in Settings › Browser.

3.Permissions

GhostNote asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change these permissions at any time in the iPadOS Settings app.

  • Microphone — to record voice notes. Recordings are saved in the app on your iPad and are not uploaded or transcribed by a server.
  • Camera — to take a photo to add to a page and to scan documents. Images are processed and stored on your device.
  • Photos — to add pictures from your library to notes or covers, and, if you choose, to save snips to your photo library.
  • Notifications — if you use a focus timer or countdown, GhostNote may ask to notify you when it ends. These are local notifications scheduled on your iPad; no push-notification service is used.

4.Handwriting and text recognition

Features such as handwriting to text, document scanning and recognising text in PDFs use Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and VisionKit). The recognition runs on your iPad; your handwriting and documents are not sent anywhere.

5.Purchases (GhostNote Pro)

GhostNote Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment; GhostNote never sees your payment details or your Apple Account credentials. The app only receives, from Apple, whether a GhostNote Pro purchase or subscription is active on your account, and keeps that status on your iPad so Pro features keep working offline. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

6.Sharing and exporting

When you export or share a notebook or page (PDF, image, print, Save to Files, AirDrop or another app), the content goes only where you send it. What happens next is governed by the receiving app or person.

7.Analytics, tracking and advertising

GhostNote contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, sends no crash reports of its own and does not track you across apps or websites. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iPadOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as crash counts) under Apple's own policy; these do not include your notes.

Diagnostic reports on your iPad. iPadOS can give apps technical reports about their own performance through Apple's MetricKit framework: hangs, crashes, launch times, battery, memory, CPU and disk-write use, and the related technical details (for example which part of the app's code was running). GhostNote saves the most recent of these reports (about 20) only on your iPad, in the app's private storage (Application Support › Diagnostics), so that a problem can be investigated if you ask for help. They contain no notes, handwriting, drawings, images, recordings, web pages or browsing history.

  • These reports are never sent automatically — not to us and not to anyone else.
  • They leave your iPad only if you choose to share them yourself in Settings › About › Diagnostics with the system share sheet (for example by e-mail to support). After that, the recipient's handling applies.
  • You can delete them at any time in the same place; deleting GhostNote removes them too. They are excluded from device backups.

8.Deleting your data

  • Delete a notebook from the library: it moves to Recently Deleted and is permanently deleted after 30 days, or immediately if you choose Delete forever or Empty trash.
  • Clear browsing history and website data in Settings › Browser.
  • Delete diagnostic reports in Settings › About › Diagnostics.
  • Deleting GhostNote from your iPad removes all of its data from the device.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored with us to delete.

9.Children

GhostNote is a general-purpose study and note-taking app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Websites opened in the in-app browser have their own policies; parents can use Screen Time to restrict web content.

10.Changes to this policy

If GhostNote's handling of information changes, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change. Significant changes will be noted in the App Store release notes.

11.Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information. All contact options are listed on the Support page.
