Photosynthesis: how plants make food

Lecture 4 · 18 min · Dr. A. Kaya

Plants capture the energy of sunlight and store it in sugar. In this lecture we follow a single leaf: how chlorophyll absorbs light, how water is split and oxygen released, and how the Calvin cycle turns carbon dioxide into glucose.
